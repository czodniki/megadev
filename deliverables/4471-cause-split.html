The shared parser reads month first

The shared date parser reads every date month first. Tenants in day-first locales then see the month and day flipped. These reports arrive through the app, email, and booking forms. For example, report R-41200 says a 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.

The parser cause covers every day-first tenant in the export. Intake tags do not reliably identify the cause: the table shows reports tagged hint that still belong to the parser group.

Track the parser cause in issue #5. The desk reports the parser fix as complete.

The booking-v3 hint tells people the wrong order

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY, but its day-first tenants need DD/MM/YYYY. This is a separate failure from the shared parser, and appears only in form reports. It affects Alder Physio, Copper Studio, and Pale Harbour. For example, report R-41230 from Copper Studio says the client came on 1 June instead of 6 January.

These form reports need their own issue, separate from the parser issue, even though the desk merged both causes into #4471.

Track the retired form-hint cause in issue #6. The desk will not fix it because booking-v3 was retired.
